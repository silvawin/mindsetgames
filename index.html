Player support

Questions, answered

How do I get help with a game?

Email us at support@silvagames.cam. Tell us which game, your device model and iOS version, and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.

Can I turn off personalized ads?

Yes. Decline when a game asks to track you, or go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone or iPad. You may still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

How do I delete my data?

Our games don't require an account. Deleting a game removes the data it stored on your device. To ask us to delete anything else we hold, such as support emails, write to support@silvagames.cam.

Which devices are supported?

Our games are made for iPhone and iPad. Each game's App Store page lists the minimum iOS version it needs.

Something not working, or have an idea?

We read every message. Write to us and include the game name and your device so we can help faster.
